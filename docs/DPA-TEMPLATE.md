# Data Processing Agreement (TEMPLATE)

> **This is a starting template, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer or your school/district counsel review and adapt it before signing. Brackets are blanks to fill in.

This agreement is between **[School name]** ("the School") and **[Provider name]** ("the Provider"), effective **[date]**.

## 1. Purpose

The Provider sets up and supports a self-hosted installation of Iridium, an open-source school portal, for the School.

## 2. Who controls the data

The School owns and controls all student, staff and parent data in the installation ("School Data"). The software runs on **[School-owned server / Provider-managed server at ___]**. The Provider does not claim any rights in School Data.

## 3. Provider's obligations

The Provider will:
- access School Data only as needed to install, maintain, back up and support the system at the School's request;
- not sell, share, or use School Data for advertising, marketing, profiling or any purpose other than providing the service;
- keep access credentials confidential and limit access to people who need it;
- notify the School within **[72 hours]** of learning of any unauthorized access to School Data;
- apply updates and security fixes within **[timeframe]**;
- follow the School's instructions for exporting or deleting School Data.

## 4. Security

The Provider will follow the measures described in SECURITY.md and docs/DEPLOYMENT.md, including HTTPS, role-based access, audit logging and regular backups. The parties acknowledge that an independent security audit has **not** been performed.

## 5. Backups and retention

Backups are taken **[frequency]**, kept for **[period]**, and stored at **[location]**. On termination, the Provider will, at the School's choice, return all School Data and securely delete all copies, including backups, within **[30 days]**.

## 6. Subprocessors

The Provider uses no subprocessors except: **[none / list hosting provider]**.

## 7. Student privacy laws

The School is responsible for determining its obligations under laws that apply to it (for example FERPA and COPPA in the US) and for any required parental notice or consent. The Provider will act as a school official under the School's direct control for the use and maintenance of School Data **[confirm with counsel]**.

## 8. Term and termination

Either party may end this agreement with **[30 days]** written notice. Sections on data return/deletion and confidentiality survive termination.
