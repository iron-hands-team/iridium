How to use club:
Note: This is for the school system, not for teaccher nor students

- Making a Club: Click on the button in the control bar on the top
- To view information inside a club: Click on the club bar.
- To Edit Information, click on the edit on the top right in the information banner. Note: Both Club Managers and School System can change club's information
- Informations that can be stored and altered in the bar: 
- Name: Make a name what people can easily search for.
- Id: I don't know. Schools can choose what it's purpose is.
- Location: Room Number or address.
- Description: What your club is about.
- Managers: There can be multiple managers.
- Members: Students. They cannot alter cclub information. Students information cannot be viewed in the app.

- Bookmarking a cclub: The left side of the club bar, click on the square. Click again to unbookmark.
